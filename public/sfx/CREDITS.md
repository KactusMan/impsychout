# Sound credits

Every section has its own sound. All of them play through one soft master (about 35% volume with a gentle low-pass filter), only after the visitor's first click, and can be muted from the header.

The softest moments (picking up and putting down hero pieces, the carousel chime that climbs one note per step, and the FAQ bell) are gentle sine tones made in the browser by `components/SoundProvider.tsx`, so they have no audio file.
