About my practice

Understanding, not diagnosing.

In a world that often demands non-stop productivity and emotional suppression, finding a genuinely non-judgmental space can feel transformative. Healing begins when we remove the shame and confusion around mental health.

My clinical work bridges rigorous science — including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT) — with genuine human warmth and cultural attunement.

You'll never be treated like a collection of symptoms or diagnostic checkmarks. Together, we untangle persistent thought loops, build a sense of safety in your body, and shape a life that feels like your own.

  • Kind, never preachy
  • Evidence-based, explained in plain language
  • Made for real life, at your own pace

How I work

Frameworks, uncomplicated

Therapeutic science explained in clear, friendly language — no cold clinical jargon or distant acronyms.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

Your thoughts shape your feelings.

Notice how your thoughts mould your feelings and physical reactions. Together, we gently test and reshape rigid automatic patterns.

“what's the evidence for that thought?”

Dialectical Behaviour Therapy

Ride the wave, don't drown in it.

Practical, actionable skills for intense emotions, tolerating deep distress without panic, and building grounded relationships.

“both things can be true.”

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Stop wrestling with your feelings.

Make room for what's present, then take clear action toward what you genuinely value.

“make space, then move.”

Rational Emotive Behaviour Therapy

Drop the hidden “musts”.

Uncover the unspoken rules and self-limiting expectations driving your mood, and build steady, unconditional self-worth.

“says who?”

Positive Behaviour Management

Environments where minds thrive.

Strengths-based approaches to ease sensory-overload triggers and shape spaces where neurodivergent minds can flourish.

“change the room, not the person.”

Eidetic Therapy

When words aren't enough.

Work with inner imagery and sensory memory to gently loosen emotional knots that talking alone can't reach.

“what do you see?”

What to expect

Starting therapy shouldn't feel scary.

Here's the gentle, four-step path from booking to steady growth.

  1. 01

    Book & reserve

    Pick a time that suits your timezone. You'll get a quick confirmation and a private session link.

  2. 02

    Intake conversation

    A gentle 50-minute first chat about where you are, what's hard and your pace. No pressure to share everything at once.

  3. 03

    A plan, together

    We co-create a clear, flexible roadmap with evidence-based tools that suit your rhythm.

  4. 04

    Ongoing growth

    Weekly or fortnightly sessions to practise skills, steady your emotions and build lasting self-trust.
